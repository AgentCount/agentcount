purpose.

You retain full ownership of everything you contribute. Nothing here assigns
copyright.

royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, reproduce, modify, publish,
sublicense and distribute your contribution, **including under licences other
than Apache-2.0**.

You also grant a patent licence on the same terms, covering any patent claim

litigation alleging that the project infringes your patent, this patent licence
terminates.

## 3. What that clause is actually for

Point 2 is the only part that matters, so it should be said directly rather
than left to be inferred: **it allows the project to be relicensed, or to add a
commercial edition, without tracking down every past contributor.**

That is a real possibility — [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) says so — and it
is stated here rather than buried, so nobody discovers it later and feels
misled. What it does **not** allow is taking anything away from you: your
contribution stays yours, the Apache-2.0 release of the code already made
cannot be revoked, and anyone who already has the code keeps it under the
licence they received it under.

## 4. What you are asserting

- The contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit it.
- If your employer has rights to work you produce, you have their permission.
- To your knowledge, it does not infringe anyone's rights.

You provide your contribution "as is", without warranty of any kind.
