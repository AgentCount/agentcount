Scoring methodology

A trust score blends four positive signals, then applies a Sybil penalty as a multiplier. The penalty is the whole point: no amount of payment, liveness, age, or reputation can rescue an agent that is clearly part of a coordinated farm. 

raw   = w_pay·payment + w_live·liveness + w_age·age + w_rep·reputation
final = raw · (1 − sybil_penalty)

The signals

Payment
Real economic activity, weighted by counterparty diversity and log-scaled — many small payments from distinct wallets beat a few big self-dealing ones.
Reputation
Feedback attestations, each discounted by the attester's own trust and by reciprocity, so a ring of agents rating each other can't bootstrap itself.
Liveness
Whether the endpoint actually responds and serves a valid agent-card, measured over repeated probes.
Age
Sustained, spread-out activity — penalising the "born yesterday, 500 ratings by lunch" pattern.
Sybil penalty
Derived from clustering agents by shared funding, shared operator, synchronised registration, and reciprocal-feedback density. Scales the whole score down.
